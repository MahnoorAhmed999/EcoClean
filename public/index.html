How It Works

1

Create Account

Sign up in under 2 minutes with your name, address, and a secure password.

2

Schedule Pickup

Choose your waste type, preferred date, and time slot. Set recurring pickups for convenience.

Features

Everything you need for hassle-free waste management

Smart Scheduling

Book one-time or recurring pickups (weekly/monthly) with flexible time slots that fit your lifestyle.

Live Status Tracking

Watch your booking go from Pending → Assigned → Completed in real-time with instant updates.

Zone-Based Service

Workers are assigned by zone — Satellite Town, Bahria Town, Chaklala, and more — for fast service.

Priority Members

Regular users (10+ bookings) get Priority status with faster scheduling and dedicated support.

Multiple Waste Types

Handle Household, Recyclable, Organic, Electronic, and Hazardous waste with type-specific collection.

Impact Dashboard

See your environmental impact — kg of waste collected, CO₂ saved, and your contribution to a cleaner city.
